# Paperhue 0.4.2: bluer skies under Natural

## Intent

On the device, the owner found the blue skies of two photo pages 「太灰白，不夠藍」 (too grey-white, not blue enough)
with 自然 / Natural. Two calendar pages with warm photos were fine and must stay as they are.

Research (2026-10-15/16, on the owner's pictures, kept out of the repo):
- A bright sky blue such as (118,209,251) lies about 12 times outside the panel's colours at its own lightness. The
  panel's blue and green inks are dark, so at that lightness the panel can only add a few blue and green dots to
  white. Natural keeps lightness, so it kept only 8% of the colour: on the device the sky measured OKLCh chroma 0.013
  against 0.099 on screen, and it was a little lighter than the original (L 0.84 against 0.81).
- The panel already shows the most colour it can at that lightness. The only way to more blue is less lightness.
- A device test of four versions (lightness traded for colour at strength 0.75 and 1.0, and a variant turned towards
  the blue ink) gave the owner's choice: 「2~3 之間比較靠近 3」, between 0.75 and 1.0, nearer 1.0.
- Applying the move by "how far outside" alone also darkened faint tints near white (a bluish white wall, pale pink),
  because near white the panel can show almost no colour. Scaling the move by the pixel's own colourfulness fixes it.

## Goals

1. **The cusp move** in Natural step A (`shared/DITHER.md` §4): a colour outside the panel's colours at its own
   lightness is aimed from a grey between its lightness and the lightness where its hue is most colourful.
   - Strength CUSP 0.9 (the owner's choice), full from SPAN 2 beyond the hull, and with the square of the pixel's
     colourfulness against the hue's most colourful panel colour, full at CREF 3 times it.
   - On the owner's skies this gives OKLCh L 0.67, C 0.035 (version 2 was L 0.70, C 0.031; version 3 L 0.64,
     C 0.041). Near-white tints hardly move, and colours the panel can show keep their lightness. Of the two warm
     calendar pages, page 3 at its saved crop comes out about 1.7% darker in average colour (almost half its dots
     change; the light-blue shirt turns denim, L 0.84 to 0.68, and its folds lose over half their contrast); the
     other crop changes by 0.1%. The owner should check page 3 on the device before release.
2. **All three apps give the same bytes**, pinned by `shared/vectors/natural-vectors.json` version 2.
3. **Classic is unchanged** (`dither-vectors.json` untouched), and so are the previews' rules, the grain levels and
   the settings.

## Non-goals

- No new colour choice: Natural changes; Classic stays the old look.
- No palette calibration (still the datasheet colours).
- No change to the softer grain levels.

## Rulings (my calls; the owner can overrule)

- **No 「自然（舊）」 option.** The owner asked for Natural itself to change after testing it on the device. Setting
  CUSP to 0 gives exactly the 0.4.0 output (the anchor is then the plain grey), so the old look can come back as an
  option later if the owner wants it. Cost if wrong: one more picker entry later.
- **SPAN 2 and CREF 3** are mine: they keep the tested sky look (within 0.01 OKLab L of the strength the owner chose)
  while faint tints hardly move. Cost if wrong: two numbers in the tables and re-pinned vectors.
- **Version 0.4.2**: what reaches the device changes, but no setting or feature does.
- **What is already on the device** changes only when it is sent again: photo pages and cards by pushing again,
  calendar backgrounds at their next sync. Any cache of dithered pictures must not hand out a 0.4.1 result.

## Risks

- **Darker colours:** vivid light colours (skies, light greens, pinks, lavender) come out darker and more
  colourful. That is the point, but a picture of mostly vivid pastels gets heavier.
- **The real panel's colours are unknown** (datasheet values). The owner judged on the device, which is the real test.
