# Paperhue v0.4.0: natural colours

Status: decided with the owner on 2026-10-14, after comparing both methods on the device. The algorithm and the
numbers every app must agree on are in `shared/DITHER.md`.

## Intent

The owner said the grain looked odd in 0.3.3 (pastel picture, 標準, Mac preview), and asked for something better
than 標準. Comparing on the device:

- Today's dithering is too light overall. The owner's words: 「現在 整體太淡了」.
- The new method looks better: 「建議好一點」.
- The previews don't look like the device: 「跟預覽畫面有點落差」.
- The owner wants to keep today's look and let users choose: 「保留經典顏色選項，顏色讓使用者選」.

Why today's method looks like this (research, 2026-10-14):
- It matches against the ideal colours (pure green, blue and so on), but the panel's real inks are darker and duller.
- Pictures come out about 6 L\* too light.
- About 12% of coloured pixels get complementary specks, such as orange dots in lavender hair or green in a peach
  coat.
- The Mac preview draws every dot 1:1 in the bright ideal colours, so it looks even grainier than the device.

## Goals

1. **A colour setting, 「顏色」 / "Colours", on every app**:
   - It has two choices: **自然 / Natural**, the new method and the default, and **經典 / Classic**, today's
     method unchanged bit for bit.
   - It sits next to 顆粒感 / Grain and is global like it. Photo pages, calendar backgrounds and card photos use it.
2. **Grain works with both choices.** 標準 / 柔和 / 更柔和 / 最平滑 stay.
   - Changing to 自然 sets the grain to 標準, and the user can change it afterwards; picking 自然 again when it is
     already chosen changes nothing. The owner's words:
     「選自然的時候預設標準，但使用者要調還是可以調」.
3. **Natural is the judge's "p1d" on the panel's datasheet colours** (`shared/DITHER.md`). It works in linear light:
   - colours are mapped into what the panel can show, keeping hue and lightness;
   - each pixel uses only black, white and the two inks either side of its hue;
   - error diffusion is serpentine Stucki;
   - a mild contrast and saturation lift is applied.
4. **The previews show the device's colours.** Every app draws every frame with the measured panel colours:
   - this covers photos in both modes, calendar pages and cards;
   - when a preview is smaller than 800x480, it is shrunk by averaging in linear light.
5. **Python, TypeScript and Swift give the same bytes.** The new `shared/vectors/natural-vectors.json` pins Natural,
   and `dither-vectors.json` keeps pinning Classic, unchanged.

## Non-goals

- **No colour calibration of the owner's device yet.** The palette comes from the datasheet. When it is measured
  later, only data changes (`shared/DITHER.md` §3).
- **No change to calendar pages.** Their text and marks are drawn in exact panel colours, with no dithering.
- **No change to the protocol, crop, fit or the 4K cap.**
- **No new smoothing method in this release** (see Rulings).

## Rulings (my calls; the owner can overrule)

- **Numbers:**
  - Natural uses IEEE-754 binary64 in a fixed evaluation order, with every pow/sqrt/hull value shipped as data.
  - Python and JS already agree byte for byte on 5 full frames.
  - The Mac checked that swiftc never fuses `a*b + c` on arm64 (Swift 6.4, -Onone through -Ounchecked).
  - Cost if wrong: the spec changes to fixed point and the vectors are re-pinned.
- **Softer grain under Natural:**
  - It scales the Stucki weights, f = (w / 42) × strength, the same rule as Classic.
  - The research found that this bands, for example flat hue steps at 最平滑. It is what the owner asked for: the user
    may choose it.
  - Follow-up: rebuild the softer levels for Natural, for example blue-noise ordered dithering inside the hue sectors.
- **Upgrading:** settings without a colour load as 自然 with grain 標準. This is the owner's rule for choosing 自然,
  applied once.
  - Cost if wrong: someone who used 柔和 gets 標準 once and picks it again.
  - The release notes say so.
- **Settings key:** `"colours": "natural" | "classic"` in settings.json (Windows, Mac, CLI) and in the phone's
  settings.
- **CLI:**
  - `ulani` gets `--colours natural|classic`, with natural as the default.
  - `--no-dither` stays.
  - The Swift CLI gets the same option.
- **The preview palette is one shared table** (`shared/dither/natural-tables.json` `palette_srgb`), so a future
  calibration moves the dither and the preview together.
- **The colour choice is one global setting, like grain.** It is not per page: one picker, and the same rule for the
  scheduled sync.
- **Screenshots in the READMEs** are retaken where the preview colours changed.

## Risks

- **The panel's real colours are unknown.**
  - The datasheet allows up to ΔE00 7 per ink.
  - If the device is darker than the datasheet, Natural can look up to about 5 L\* too dark. That is still better on
    average than Classic in the cross-test.
- **Visible costs of Natural:**
  - a black/white checker in near-grey light areas;
  - a thin arc of dots where a gradient crosses a hue sector;
  - black pepper in dull mid-tones.
  - The owner saw the device test and preferred it anyway.
- **Speed:** pure Python is a few seconds per photo, like today. The phone takes about 0.2–0.5 s.
- **IP** (research, not legal advice):
  - The method's ink-set choice is HP's expired MBVQ idea, US5991438.
  - It does not do what E Ink's active US10270939 or US11721296 claim.
  - The owner was told.
